Common questions

How do I receive a reminder?

Set a future time in “Remind me” and allow Purrlane notifications in iPhone Settings. Adding a task to Today alone does not schedule an alert. If you use Work or Personal Focus, allow Purrlane in that Focus mode.

How do sound and vibration work?

Custom tones, including Mica’s meow, play ten times per alert. Preview plays one sample; the system tone follows iPhone settings. Enable notification sounds and choose the haptics behavior you want under iPhone Settings → Sounds & Haptics. Silent mode, Focus and other system settings can affect alerts. Purrlane cannot force repeated vibration.

Why did a Lock Screen task card disappear?

Each unfinished task for today can have its own Live Activity. iOS limits activity duration and the number shown; these are not permanent widgets. Open Purrlane again to refresh eligible tasks. Timed notifications are scheduled separately.

How do repeating tasks work?

Choose daily, weekdays or weekends. Weekdays means Monday through Friday and does not exclude local public holidays. Completing today’s occurrence does not stop future reminders; you do not have to mark every occurrence complete for the plan to continue.

Can I use iPhone Calendar?

Turn on “Show in iPhone Calendar” for a repeating task and allow Calendar access. Calendar events follow the account’s own sync settings. If permission is denied, your local plan remains available.

Can I switch language or appearance?

Open Purrlane Settings to choose English or Simplified Chinese, and Light, Dark or Follow system. Your own task titles are never automatically translated.

Do I need an account?

No. Task data is stored on your device. For details about Calendar, backups, notifications and support email, read our privacy policy.
