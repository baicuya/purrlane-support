Your plans. Your privacy.

Privacy policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Purrlane is an iPhone app for tasks, reminders and focus time with Mica. No account is required.

The app does not send your task content to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

Information on your device

Purrlane stores task titles and notes, dates, reminder settings, repeating plans, completion information, focus history, Mica’s nickname and your preferences locally on your iPhone. Local backup copies and diagnostic files may also be stored on the device. Widgets and Live Activities use a shared on-device snapshot to display your plans.

Your device’s backups may include app data, depending on your Apple and device settings. Purrlane does not provide its own cloud account or app-managed cloud synchronization.

Notifications and the Lock Screen

With your permission, iOS schedules local reminders. Your task name may appear in notifications, widgets or Live Activities. Turn on “Hide task content” in Purrlane Settings to use a generic message on these surfaces. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings at any time.

Optional Calendar access

If you choose to show repeating plans in iPhone Calendar, Purrlane requests Calendar access to create, update and remove the corresponding events. It stores the identifiers of events it creates and the synchronization state. Your local task remains available if you decline access.

Events are saved to a writable calendar provided by iOS. That calendar may sync through iCloud, Google, Microsoft or another account you configured. This is handled by your calendar provider, under its privacy policy and your settings. Purrlane does not upload your calendar to its own server.

Optional on-device intelligence

On supported devices, Apple’s on-device models may help tidy task wording. Manual entry remains available. Purrlane does not send this text to a third-party cloud AI service.

Support and TestFlight

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond and troubleshoot. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please avoid sending private task content or other sensitive details unless needed for your request. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable recordkeeping requirements.

If you use a TestFlight version, Apple may collect and make beta-testing diagnostics or feedback available through TestFlight under Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Purrlane does not include a separate analytics SDK.

This support website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub may log network information such as IP addresses for security when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from the app’s local task storage.

Your controls

  • Edit or delete tasks and repeating plans inside Purrlane.
  • Manage Calendar and notification permissions in iPhone Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading an app may retain its data. Copies in device backups are managed through your backup settings.
  • Calendar events already created may remain after deleting the app. Remove a synced plan while Calendar access is available, or delete its event in Calendar.
